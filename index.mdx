## What the skill does

The skill runs a structured, multi-phase workflow inside Claude Code. You provide a job description (and optionally a company name); it handles the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build your resume library">
    The skill scans your `resumes/` directory for existing Markdown resumes and indexes every experience, skill, and achievement into a searchable library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Research the role">
    Using WebSearch, the skill researches the company's mission, values, and culture, then benchmarks LinkedIn profiles of people in similar roles to build a success profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover undocumented experience">
    Through a branching conversational interview, the skill surfaces projects, volunteer work, and side activities that never made it into your existing resumes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match and score content">
    Every experience is scored against the job description using a four-factor formula (Direct, Transferable, Adjacent, Impact). Gaps are identified and transparent options provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate multi-format output">
    The skill produces a tailored Markdown resume, DOCX, PDF, and an interview prep report — all in one pass.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core principle

> **Your ability to get a job should be based on your experiences and capabilities, not on your resume writing skills.**

This skill exists to close the gap between what you have actually accomplished and how well a resume communicates it to a hiring manager.
